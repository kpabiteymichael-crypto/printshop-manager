PROJECT: PRINTING PRESS + BOOKSTORE + STATIONERY BUSINESS MANAGEMENT APP

OBJECTIVE

Build a production-ready, offline-first business management application for a Printing Press, Photocopy Center, Bookstore, and Stationery Shop.

The application must run seamlessly on:

- Android Phones
- Android Tablets
- Windows Desktop
- Progressive Web App (PWA)

The system should be optimized for small and medium businesses in Ghana and support both online and offline operation. When internet connectivity is unavailable, all transactions must continue to work locally and synchronize automatically when connectivity is restored.

The application should be designed as a modular ERP combining POS, inventory, printing operations, cash management, customer management, analytics, and AI-driven business insights.

====================================================

====================================================

Frontend:
- Flutter (preferred)
- Responsive UI
